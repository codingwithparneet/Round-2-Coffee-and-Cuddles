The Founders’ Journey

Two Decades, Sixty Countries, One Table

Your hosts are Digvijay Kaushal and Vaishnavi Sharma. A married couple who met over library books at JNU, traveled the globe for twenty years in the diplomatic service, and have returned to Dehradun to brew with stillness and intent.

Digvijay Kaushal

Diplomat & Brewer

“I grew up in Malaud, a small town in Punjab where the biggest adventure was the bus to Ludhiana. At 18 I reached JNU in Delhi to study international relations, with one suitcase and a lot of nerves. In my first week, a girl from Dehradun in the library corrected my reading list. That was Vaishnavi.”

Vaishnavi Sharma

Historian & Photographer

“I was studying history and spending more time with a camera than with my books. Digvijay was the quiet one at the Ganga Dhaba who could argue about foreign policy for hours and forget to eat. After three years of late-night chai, we were inseparable.”

Digvijay Kaushal

Indian Foreign Service

“I cleared the civil services exam and joined the Indian Foreign Service. For twenty years we lived wherever the government posted me: Addis Ababa, Rome, Hanoi, Bogota and more. Vaishnavi came to every one and turned each posting into a photo series.”

Vaishnavi Sharma

Curator

“Between postings and holidays we visited more than sixty countries, and in each one our day began the same way: finding the best cup of coffee in town. A tiny stall in Addis Ababa, where beans are roasted in front of you and served with incense. A quiet bar in Trieste. A rooftop in Medellin. A farmhouse in Coorg at six in the morning.”

Digvijay Kaushal

Roaster

“Somewhere along the way, coffee stopped being a drink and became our way of understanding a place. Every cup has a farmer, a mountain and a story behind it. We started carrying a hand grinder, then a scale, then a small roaster, and we learned from growers in Ethiopia, Colombia, Vietnam and Yemen.”

Vaishnavi Sharma

Dehradun Native

“After twenty years, Digvijay took voluntary retirement from the service and we asked ourselves where we wanted to stop. The answer was easy. Dehradun is my hometown, with the hills, the cool mornings and the unhurried pace we spent two decades looking for. It is also a short drive from Mussoorie, where Digvijay spent his first year of training.”

Digvijay Kaushal

Co-Founder

“Coffee and Cuddles is our small corner of the world, open this winter on Rajpur Road. Every coffee on our menu is inspired by a place we have loved. We want guests to slow down, sit by the window and taste somewhere new.”
